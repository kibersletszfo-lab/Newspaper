# Newspaper World News

**0.5.0 Beta for Victoria 3 1.15.0**

A national newspaper with monthly issues, breaking editions and an archive. Follow the changes in your campaign through reports on life at home and news from abroad.

Created by **274 — BTTO Modding**. Published by **kibersletszfo-lab**.

![Newspaper World News](thumbnail.png)

## What you can read

- **Economy** — living standards, shortages, infrastructure, market access, production achievements and follow-up reports when earlier problems improve. Quiet months can bring a market price review.
- **War and diplomacy** — wars, alliances, power blocs and developments affecting your partners. Diplomatic crisis reports cover the start of a confrontation, backing down and new participants.
- **Society** — laws, literacy, elections and scientific advances. Coverage includes all 148 laws in Victoria 3 1.15.0 and nine selected technologies. Election reports use available party momentum data.
- **World events** — changes in international standing and 16 stories with dedicated breaking editions, including German and Italian unification, the American Civil War, and the completion of the Suez and Panama canals.

Articles combine prewritten passages with facts recorded in your campaign. Relations, diplomatic ties and the importance of a country influence which foreign stories reach your newspaper and how the editorial desk discusses them.

## Subscribe and read

Choose monthly issues and breaking editions independently. Your first subscription brings a welcome issue introducing all four sections.

An unread badge on the newspaper button announces regular issues. Breaking editions cover one important event, open automatically and pause the game when pause control is available.

The paper includes national mastheads, engravings, a cover with links to articles and two-column reading pages. Long articles continue onto further sheets. The archive holds the latest 12 issues and remembers your reading position during the current session.

## Installation

1. Download **Newspaper_0.5.0_Victoria_3_1.15.0.zip** from [Releases](https://github.com/kibersletszfo-lab/Newspaper/releases).
2. Extract the ZIP into your Victoria 3 user mod folder, usually `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Victoria 3/mod/`. The result should be `mod/Newspaper/.metadata/metadata.json`.
3. Add or select the local mod in the Paradox launcher and enable it in a playset for Victoria 3 **1.15.0**.
4. Start a new campaign, open the round newspaper button near the time controls or the corresponding decision, then choose your subscriptions.

If the launcher requires a local-mod entry, create one named Newspaper with directory Newspaper, then copy the extracted contents into that directory and enable the entry in your playset.

## Beta status and compatibility

This is a prerelease. Delivery, article selection, pagination and localization continue to receive testing and corrections.

Older saves are unsupported. A new campaign is recommended. Other mods that replace the same interface windows, particularly the event window, may conflict. Multiplayer and achievement compatibility require further verification.

Report bugs through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kibersletszfo-lab/Newspaper/issues). Include game and mod versions, language, article headline, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot or relevant fresh error-log excerpt. Review logs for personal information before sharing them.
